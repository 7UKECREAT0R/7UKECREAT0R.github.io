Ability Warden Documentation Help

Ban Player

Bans a player in-game permanently.

Usage

  • /aw-ban <target: Player> <reason: Text>

Parameter Details:

  • <target: Player> The player to ban.

  • <reason: Text> The reason for the ban. All bans must have some kind of reason attached, even if it's short.

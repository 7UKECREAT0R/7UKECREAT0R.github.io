Ban Player Temporarily

Bans a player in-game temporarily. The ban will automatically be lifted after the specified number of days have passed.

Usage

  • /aw-tempban <target: Player> <days: Integer> <reason: Text>

Parameter Details:

  • <target: Player> The player to ban.

  • <days: Integer> The number of days to ban the player for.

  • <reason: Text> The reason for the ban. All bans must have some kind of reason attached, even if it's short.
